# Tips for organizing

Open Arc keeps your tabs in the sidebar, grouped by Space. A few habits keep it tidy.

- **Spaces for each part of your life.** Make one Space per project or role (work, home, a class)
  from the dock's + or Spaces ▸ New Space…. Switch with ⌃1–⌃9 or by swiping the sidebar.
- **Pinned tabs for what you return to.** Drag a tab above the divider (or press ⌘D) to pin it.
  Pinned tabs stay in their Space; Today tabs below the divider are archived after a while.
- **Favorites for every Space.** Drag a page into the favorites grid at the top of the sidebar to
  keep it in every Space of the same Profile.
- **Folders for groups.** Space menu ▸ New Folder, then drag tabs into it. Folders can nest.
- **Clear Today.** "Clear" on the divider (⇧⌘K) archives the Today tabs at once; find them later in
  Archive ▸ View Archive….
- **Split View for side by side.** Drag a tab onto the page, or use "Open in Split View" from a
  link's menu.
- **Profiles for separate logins.** Give a Space its own Profile to keep cookies and history apart
  (see [Profiles](profiles.md)).
